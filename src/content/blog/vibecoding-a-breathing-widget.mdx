<p>Vibecoding a Breathing widget</p>
<p>As somebody who spends a lot of time at their computer, I often forget to breathe. I wished I had a reminder, like a little pulsing sphere or something, that reminded me to always breathe in and out.</p>
<p>I searched online and didn’t find anything quite like what I wanted. Turns out it was the perfect opportunity to vibecode something.</p>
